## Platform

web

## Users
- Cold traffic from Meta ads, on mobile, in peninsular Spain and the Balearic Islands.
- Avatar A, «Lola»: woman aged 52–68 who sleeps on her side. She puts an ordinary pillow between her knees and it falls off during the night.
- Avatar B: son or daughter aged 35–55 looking for a useful Christmas present for their parents.
- Secondary: hikers aged 45–65.
- Details in `viabilidad/ZENTIA_AVATARES_ANGULOS.md`.

## Product Purpose
Zentia (provisional name) sells the «Kit Dormir de Lado»:
- a memory-foam cushion that sits between the knees so side sleepers keep their knees apart and hips level through the night;
- a 7-night side-sleeping guide (PDF);
- a printable gift card;
- a 60-night trial.

Success: sell the first 10 units (real supplier stock) at a CPA of 27 € or less.

## Positioning
- The honest Spanish version of a category that elsewhere in Europe runs on fake doctors, «-60%» and sciatica claims.
- Real stock count, real Christmas delivery deadline, price anchor = the real single-unit price.
- Ships from Spain in 2–5 working days.

## Brand Commitments
- Name «Zentia». Logo: cloud-shaped mark (owner request, 17-nov).
- Copy in Spanish, close and plain.
- Banned: medical claims («alivia ciática», «ortopédica», «cura»), invented reviews or ratings, invented strike-through prices, fake countdowns, AI people presented as customers.
- The owner rejected the previous look (navy night, Literata + Atkinson).

## Product Principles
1. Answer the number-one objection («se cae por la noche») in the first screen.
2. Only real urgency: real stock, real deadline, the anchor of the real single price.
3. Make the bundle the obvious choice for couples and gifts.
4. Explain with drawings, not with medical claims.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Audience 45+: large body text (17px or more), high contrast, big tap targets (48px or more), no fast flashing (the stock dot pulses slowly; reduced motion is respected).
